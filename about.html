From a Three-Year Product Development Journey to a Growing Ghanaian Food Brand.

Arn's Food and More Enterprise is a Ghanaian food-processing and product-development company established in 2025, built on research, patience, and a belief that Ghana's own ingredients deserve a place on the modern supermarket shelf.

Company Overview

Why We Started

Arn's Food and More Enterprise is a Ghanaian food processing and packaging company established in 2025, with commercial production commencing on 14 June 2026. Founded by Arnold Mawuli Komla Adjei, the company was born out of a vision to create high-value, affordable, and healthy food products using locally sourced Ghanaian agricultural ingredients.

Growing up, Arnold often wondered why many of the foods Ghanaians proudly eat every day were rarely found neatly packaged and professionally presented on supermarket shelves. Ghana has exceptional local foods, ingredients and flavours yet consumers often encounter imported alternatives that are more readily available and professionally packaged.

That question became the seed of this business: take everyday Ghanaian foods, package them differently, add value through innovation and quality, and make them products people can confidently pick up from any supermarket shelf.

We pioneered HELLO Hot Shito Powder an innovative, oil-free, dry powdered alternative to traditional shito that solves the long-standing challenge of short shelf life and messy oil leaks during travel.

A Lesson In Packaging

From Bottle to Pouch

Good taste alone doesn't build a brand. Packaging has to work too.

At the beginning of the product development journey, HELLO Hot Shito Powder was packaged in bottles. The bottle was practical but as the team learned more about the food market, consumer preferences, transportation and shelf presentation, it became clear that having a good product was only one part of building a successful food brand.

Questions like "How will customers carry it? How will it look on a shelf? Can it reduce transportation costs? Does it reflect the HELLO brand identity?" led to a deliberate decision: move from bottle packaging to premium black stand-up pouches.

The change wasn't cosmetic it was a business decision based on convenience, presentation, transportation, production efficiency, market positioning and scalability. The pouch format is lighter, more modern, and gives the brand flexibility to create different sizes for different categories of consumers.

From Idea to Commercial Production

The Journey Behind the Brand

Building a food company from the ground up has not been easy from formulation to FDA approval to a market-ready product.

2022 – 2025

Three Years of Research & Development

Different formulations of HELLO Hot Shito Powder were researched, refined and tested for taste, consistency, food safety, shelf stability, consumer acceptance, processing methods and commercial viability.

Asogli State Youth Empowerment Programme

Shortlisted Among the Final 8 of 247 Applicants

Arn's Food and More Enterprise entered the youth empowerment award competition and was shortlisted among the final eight competitors out of 247 applicants going on to win a GHS 20,000.00 award. The funding strengthened product development, sampling, regulatory processes and FDA preparations. We remain grateful to Togbe Afede XIV, the Asogli State, the Asogli State Youth Empowerment Programme and LIFE-MAC Africa.

Batch AFME-HHSP-250801-001 · March 2026

FDA Testing & Regulatory Approval

Following regulatory testing and assessment, HELLO Hot Shito Powder received approval from the Food and Drugs Authority (FDA) of Ghana in March 2026 FDA Registration Number: FDA/SC 26-1041.

Batch AFME-HHSP-260614-002 · 14 June 2026

First Commercial Production

Following regulatory approval, Arn's Food and More Enterprise commenced commercial production of HELLO Hot Shito Powder for public distribution moving from years of research and development into the marketplace.
